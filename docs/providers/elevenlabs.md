# 🔊 ElevenLabs (Audio Generation API)

Sound Lab utilizes ElevenLabs' advanced audio models to generate custom sound effects (SFX) directly inside Unity.

---

## 🔑 Setup Instructions

1.  Sign up for an account at [elevenlabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io).
2.  Navigate to your account profile settings and select **API Keys**.
3.  Generate a new API key and copy it.
4.  Open Unity and navigate to **Tools > Brody AI > Tool Settings**.
5.  Go to the **Sound Lab** tab.
6.  Paste your key in the **ElevenLabs API Key** field.
7.  Verify by opening **Tools > Brody AI > Sound Lab** and generating a test sound clip.

---

## 🆓 Free Quotas
*   ElevenLabs' free plan grants **10,000 credits/month**.
*   Generating a short SFX clip typically costs around **100 credits**, meaning you can generate roughly **100 individual clips** every month for free.
*   Once your monthly credits run out, you will need to wait for the monthly billing cycle reset or upgrade your subscription plan on ElevenLabs' website.
