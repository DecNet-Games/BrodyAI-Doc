# 📎 Smart Context References (Drag & Drop)

Instead of relying on Brody to guess which scripts or assets you are talking about, you can use the **Smart Reference System** to feed precise files directly to the AI.

---

## 🚀 How to Use

1.  Open the **Brody AI** chat window (**Tools > Brody AI**).
2.  Select any asset in your **Project folder** (e.g. a `.cs` script, a shader, a material) or a **GameObject** in the Hierarchy.
3.  Drag and drop it directly onto the Brody AI chat window.
4.  The asset will appear as a badge in the **📎 Context** bar located just above the text input.
5.  Type your question or request. Brody will read the exact contents of the attached files to formulate its answer.
6.  To clear a reference, simply click the **✕** button on its context badge.
