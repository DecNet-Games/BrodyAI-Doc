# Brody AI
## Your Agentic Developing Homie 🤖

**Stop struggling with manual code fixes and context-switching. Brody AI is a senior-level autonomous developer agent that lives inside your Unity Editor.**

Designed for professional developers who value their time, Brody AI doesn't just chat—it scans your codebase, analyzes project dependencies, generates production-ready scripts, edits your hierarchy, and handles audio/material assets automatically.

## ⚡ The Brody Advantage

Brody is built to solve the "Context Gap" that web-based AI tools struggle with. Because he lives inside your active Unity Editor session, he has real-time awareness of your scripts, scene hierarchy, open errors, and project folder structures.

### 🚀 High-Impact Workflow
- **Senior Architect**: Plan and scaffold entire game systems with abstract classes, managers, and interfaces.
- **Auto-Repair Man**: Single-click diagnostic scan and source-code patching for console errors.
- **Asset Creator**: Generate high-quality PBR textures and game-ready SFX clips directly to your Assets folder.
- **100% Customization**: Use standard cloud LLMs, local offline models (Ollama), or custom API endpoints.
